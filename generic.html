3T3 Swiss Albino Cell Culturing

I investigated cell attachment, proliferation, and cytotoxicity in tissue engineering. I conducted pipette accuracy testing, cell attachment assays with agarose-gelatin hydrogels, and cytotoxicity assays to assess material biocompatibility. I analyzed growth curves and attachment rates using R, providing insights into scaffold optimization and biomaterial compatibility.

Skills: Cell Culture · Aseptic Technique · Biomaterials
